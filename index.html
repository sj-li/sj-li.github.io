Hi, I’m Shijie. I am a Scientist at A*STAR in Singapore, working on embodied intelligence and multimodal foundation models. I am the Principal Investigator of an A*STAR Career Development Fund project (2025–2028). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Bonn, advised by Prof. Juergen Gall, and my M.S. from Nankai University, advised by Prof. Ming-Ming Cheng. Previously, I was a research intern at Qualcomm AI Research in Amsterdam and Intel Labs in Munich.

My research is in computer vision and 3D perception, with robots as a platform for learning and acting in the physical world. My goal is to build robots that learn from their own deployment: understanding what happened, deciding what to update, and retaining a lesson only while it remains valid. This work brings together three directions:

Spatial perception and reasoning: Grounding decisions in 3D scenes and world representations.

I study how to reconstruct, understand, and reason about the physical world, from large-scale 3D perception to language-guided grounding. A central question is how to connect observations to the right object or event, and gather further evidence when the cause of a failure is unclear. Representative work: SeeGround, MonoSR, and One Agent to Guide Them All.

Temporal understanding and memory: Recognising the past, anticipating the future, and knowing what to retain.

I model how activities and scenes unfold over time, connecting action segmentation, forecasting, and memory for long-horizon reasoning. I am interested in how robots can track changing conditions and determine when an earlier lesson should be re-verified, revised, or withdrawn. Representative work: MS-TCN++, Thinking Ahead, and PRISM.

Efficient, cost-aware embodied intelligence: Choosing when to look, ask, act, and learn.

I develop embodied systems that weigh the value of additional evidence against the cost of sensing, computation, and human help. This extends to deciding whether a deployment experience calls for an update to memory, a reusable skill, or model weights, with verification and rollback when needed. Representative work: SpatialReasoner, Talk2Escape, and Ask When It Pays.
